常见问题 Frequently Asked Questions

  • 登记完会有人来找我吗？ Will anyone get in touch after I register?

    会的。你登记之后，信息会出现在首页的公告栏里，有需要的同学看到就会来联系你。 Yes. After you register, your details appear in the notice list on the home page. Students who need the book will get in touch.

  • 教材可以便宜点卖吗？ Can I sell my textbooks for less?

    可以。教材的话，写个低于原价一半的价格比较好卖；其他书建议按原价换，不亏也不赚。 Yes. For textbooks, a price below half of the original one usually sells faster. Other books are best swapped at the full price.

  • 见面交接安全吗？ Is it safe to meet up in person?

    尽量约在图书馆、学生中心这种人来人往的地方，最好再叫上一个同学一起去。 Try to meet in a busy place such as the Library or the Student Centre, and it is better to bring a friend along.
