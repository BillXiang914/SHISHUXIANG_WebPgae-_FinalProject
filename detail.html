购书小贴士 Buying Tips

  1. 有笔记和划线的教材，价格通常比全新低 15 元起 Textbooks with notes and highlighting usually go for 15 CNY less than a brand-new copy.
  2. 交接前先拍一下书本的整体和边角状态 Take a photo of the whole book and of its corners before the handover.
  3. 同一科目的教材最好统一在固定时间点交换 It works best to arrange the exchange of textbooks for the same course at one fixed time.
  4. 购买前先确认是否有缺页或破损 Always check for missing or damaged pages before you accept the book.
