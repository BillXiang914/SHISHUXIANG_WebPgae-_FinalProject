关于本站 About This Site

本站由在校学生自发维护，所有图书信息均由同学自主登记，不收取任何中间费用。 This site is maintained by students. Every book listed here is registered by a fellow student, and no fee is charged at any point.
书籍流转只发生在校园内，以当面交接为主，登记时会在留言中写清楚时间和地点。 Books only change hands inside the campus, and handover is done face to face. When you register, please state the time and the meeting point clearly in your message.

交换流程 How an Exchange Works

  1. 在留言板登记你要出手或想换的书 Register the book you want to sell or swap on the Message Board page.
  2. 等待站内同学联系，约定交换时间 Wait for another student to contact you and agree on a handover time.
  3. 在指定地点当面交接，当场确认书本状态 Meet at the agreed place and check the condition of the book together.
  4. 教材可以折价转让，其他书籍建议原价交换 Textbooks may be transferred at a discount; other books are best exchanged at full price.

交换守则 Exchange Guidelines

01

书页完整 Complete Pages

缺页、严重折角的书请在留言里注明，免得后面有误会。 If a book is missing pages or badly creased, say so in your message to avoid confusion later.

02

不代替教材 No Textbooks on Your Behalf

教材直接找同学换，我们不代收，也不代寄。 Swap textbooks with a student directly. We do not collect or post books for you.

03

不强行定价 No Fixed Price

价格由买卖双方商量，登记时写个参考价就够了。 You and the other student agree on the price. A reference price in the listing is enough.

04

按时出现 Show Up on Time

约定好后尽量准时，有变动提前在留言里说一声。 Try to be on time. If something changes, post a note on the message board first.

手上有读完的书？登记一下，让它去到需要的人手里。 Finished a book? List it and let it reach the student who needs it.

常见问题 FAQ

  • 教材什么时候最好换？ When is the best time to swap textbooks?

    开学前两周和期末考完最忙，建议提前一周登记。 The busiest times are before term starts and after the final exams. Register a week ahead.

  • 可以只换不卖吗？ Can I only exchange books without selling?

    可以，交换方式选「置换」即可，全程免费。 Yes. Pick "Swap" as the exchange type. It is free.

  • 毕业了还能登记吗？ Can I still register after graduation?

    可以登记，约在图书馆或学生中心交接。 Yes, you are welcome to register. Meet at the Library or the Student Centre.

  • 如何保证书本品相？ How is the condition of a book guaranteed?

    登记时写清书页、封面和批注，交接时当面确认。 Describe the pages, cover and annotations when you register, then check the book together.
